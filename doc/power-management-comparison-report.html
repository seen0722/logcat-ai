13. Conclusion

Cross-comparison of three bugreports reveals systemic power management issues on the Trimble T70:

  1. Deep Doze discharge rate exceeds ideal in all scenarios — 50.5 mAh/h without WiFi (ideal <20), 79.9–93.0 mAh/h with WiFi
  2. timerfd is the primary and consistent suspend abort cause (72–94%), though the root likely spans multiple userspace components
  3. WiFi background traffic is the largest single factor in short-term discharge rate spikes, contributing 30–42 mAh/h additional power consumption
  4. Modem EM7590 wake behavior is a consistent hardware-level issue (avg 11.6–12.2s per wake)
  5. BatteryStats power profile is severely inaccurate (91–98% unaccounted), limiting software-level precise diagnostics
  6. Doze mechanism operates correctly (settings consistent, cycles stable); the problem lies in hardware activity that Doze cannot control
Most Valuable Next Step: Collect bugreports using userdebug build under both WiFi-connected and WiFi-disconnected conditions, and use dumpsys netstats --uid to identify the WiFi traffic source during Deep Doze.
